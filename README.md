## Amendments affecting eligibility and RQ3

Under the fifth eligibility criterion in force on 16 November 2026, the original article had to report a link between a support practice and an outcome for the team or a member. RQ3 already asked which outcomes study authors linked to support. [Version 1.16](https://github.com/kdmsnr/research_systematic-review_team-support/blob/c11be8783a14ba0228633c0eaa5f055531d17134/protocol.md)

- **17 November.** After full-text assessment, extraction, and initial coding, the review lead added a sixth criterion to judge whether the study methods supported interpretation of a reported link. A serious methodological problem could exclude the report if it undermined the link throughout the study. A problem confined to one result could leave other results usable. [Version 1.17](https://github.com/kdmsnr/research_systematic-review_team-support/commit/cfa27f37a27a568af02db33109fc9c177dc5efe3)
- **22–23 November.** On 22 November, Codex revisited provisionally eligible reports that had contributed no descriptions. It checked whether the original passages and the appraised study components supported every practice and outcome in each coded description. On 23 November, the review team used the existing codes to compare outcomes reported for people and for the work in accounts of support directed solely to people or solely to the work. Descriptions combining both kinds of support remained in the full table. Version 1.18 documented both decisions after the analyses. Version 1.19 then presented the procedures, including the counting rules, without the observed totals. [Version 1.18](https://github.com/kdmsnr/research_systematic-review_team-support/commit/6af79c7dc19f0b7ff9821c21f3a16389756c0382) · [Version 1.19](https://github.com/kdmsnr/research_systematic-review_team-support/commit/9f08b74700c1194738fb83277a70e84cf1b1c631)
- **24 November.** The team applied the same check to descriptions from reports already represented when the link to eligible study components remained uncertain. Version 1.20 recorded this extension and clarified which claims the source material and relevant appraisal items could support. The supplement records the effect on inclusion. [Version 1.20](https://github.com/kdmsnr/research_systematic-review_team-support/commit/d5d36ef4517d3b73e6dfa345024a0ec53575261b) · [Supplement on 24 November](https://github.com/kdmsnr/research_systematic-review_team-support/commit/d538bd819c87fd938ee3fb9262ac74c12d89c4da)
