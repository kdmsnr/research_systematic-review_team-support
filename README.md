# Systematic Review of Support for Product Development Teams

This repository hosts the protocol for *What Practitioners Notice and How They Interpret It When Supporting Product Development Teams*, a qualitative systematic review.

[Read the protocol](protocol/protocol.md) — version 1.2, dated 22 October 2026. It specifies the research questions, eligibility criteria, complete search strategies, AI-assisted assessment of all title-and-abstract records, human validation and exclusion audits, human full-text eligibility decisions, source-verified extraction, methodological appraisal, thematic synthesis and confidence assessment.

Version 1.2 limits eligible evidence reports to peer-reviewed journal articles and peer-reviewed full conference papers, including eligible conference papers published in chapter form. Theses, books and ordinary book chapters are outside scope. Future Google Scholar updates use the eight general queries at 200 positions each. All saved initial search observations and the documented 35-coordinate shortfall remain recorded.

Supplementary materials will be added only when required by reporting guidelines or the target journal.
