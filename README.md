# Systematic Review of Support for Product Development Teams

This repository hosts the protocol for *Practitioner Noticing, Support Practices and Reported Consequences in Product Development Teams*, a qualitative systematic review.

[Read the protocol](protocol/protocol.md) — version 1.7, dated 25 October 2026. It specifies the research questions, eligibility criteria, complete search strategies, bounded abstract retrieval and availability checks, AI-assisted assessment of all remaining title-and-abstract records, human validation and exclusion audits, human full-text eligibility decisions, source-verified extraction, methodological appraisal, thematic synthesis and confidence assessment.

Eligible evidence reports are peer-reviewed journal articles reporting empirical qualitative studies or separable qualitative components of mixed-methods research. Title-and-abstract assessment checks journal status and an empirical basis; established non-journal or non-empirical reports are excluded. Theory, opinion and review-only syntheses without a separable empirical study are outside scope. A review label alone is not an exclusion, and unclear cases remain for resolution. AI-proposed exclusions undergo the same human validation and audit. Conference papers, theses, books and book chapters are outside scope. Future Google Scholar updates use the eight general queries at 200 positions each. All saved initial search observations and the documented 35-coordinate shortfall remain recorded.

Version 1.3 requires a usable abstract after the specified bounded metadata-retrieval procedure. Reports for which none is obtained are excluded for abstract availability, separately from content-based AI exclusions and full-text retrieval failures. This restriction may omit otherwise eligible reports. A recovered but unresolved abstract remains pending, and later recovery reopens an availability exclusion. Human reference validation and exclusion audits apply to the records passing this check; they remain uncompleted.

Version 1.4 centers the question and analysis on reported connections among noticing, contextual conditions, interpretation and support practices. Background setting is distinguished from contextual conditions used in practitioner judgment. Explicit interpretations and specific support choices remain optional; eligibility, search expressions and title-and-abstract screening rules are unchanged.

Version 1.6 defines the target positively as empirical journal studies, superseding the blanket review-article exclusion introduced in version 1.5. The search expressions, abstract-retrieval rule, substantive C1–C4 criteria and synthesis methods remain unchanged. Use of a nearby review as a citation-search seed does not establish evidence eligibility.

Supplementary materials will be added only when required by reporting guidelines or the target journal.

Version 1.7 adds reported consequences to the question and source-linked analysis. Every included report will be examined for relevant consequences irrespective of outcome wording in its abstract. Consequence reporting and a complete noticing–support–consequence sequence are not additional eligibility requirements. The amendment follows exploratory AI analysis and precedes the revised analysis; it does not convert prior candidates into human-confirmed findings.
