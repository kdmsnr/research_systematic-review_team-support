# Provisional supplementary material

The synthesis includes descriptions that met the six-condition review criteria. A report with an eligible component could contribute only those descriptions whose support and outcomes were attributable to eligible components. Descriptions involving an ineligible or unresolved component, or lacking a complete match to eligible components, were excluded. Characteristics describe each report or case and can extend beyond the findings used in the synthesis. The review lead confirmed exclusion of the 30 reports with an unresolved report-level assessment. Individual sixth-criterion assessments include provisional judgements.

The 110 reports contributed 234 descriptions. In 50 reports, other descriptions were excluded under the component assessment rule. One further description from Shen et al. was excluded because the support function of the reported assignments could not be established.

The files below accompany the systematic review of support for development teams. Report numbers correspond across the report characteristics and JBI appraisal files. Reports are not assumed to be independent studies.

- [Search strategies and information sources](search.md)
- [Included reports and their characteristics](included-reports.md)
- [JBI appraisal decisions and scope](jbi-appraisals.md)

Details of individual JBI item rationales and the full description-level coding index are not reproduced in these files.

## Source locations for the selected comparison

The review compared accounts of support and its consequences across all 110 included reports. Of the four accounts below, the first three directly informed the finding about how contributors experienced the handling of their ideas. The fourth illustrates its boundary. How typical these experiences were across the reports is unknown. Page numbers are physical PDF pages. The accounts concern different people and situations and do not establish a common causal pathway.
