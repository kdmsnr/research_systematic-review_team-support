# Systematic Review of Support for Product Development Teams

This repository hosts the protocol for *Practitioner Noticing, Interpretation and Support Practices in Product Development Teams*, a qualitative systematic review.

[Read the protocol](protocol/protocol.md) — version 1.4, dated 22 October 2026. It specifies the research questions, eligibility criteria, complete search strategies, bounded abstract retrieval and availability checks, AI-assisted assessment of all remaining title-and-abstract records, human validation and exclusion audits, human full-text eligibility decisions, source-verified extraction, methodological appraisal, thematic synthesis and confidence assessment.

Eligible evidence reports are limited to peer-reviewed journal articles and peer-reviewed full conference papers, including eligible conference papers published in chapter form. Theses, books and ordinary book chapters are outside scope. Future Google Scholar updates use the eight general queries at 200 positions each. All saved initial search observations and the documented 35-coordinate shortfall remain recorded.

Version 1.3 requires a usable abstract after the specified bounded metadata-retrieval procedure. Reports for which none is obtained are excluded for abstract availability, separately from content-based AI exclusions and full-text retrieval failures. This restriction may omit otherwise eligible reports. A recovered but unresolved abstract remains pending, and later recovery reopens an availability exclusion. Human reference validation and exclusion audits apply to the records passing this check; they remain uncompleted.

Version 1.4 centers the question and analysis on reported connections among noticing, contextual conditions, interpretation and support practices. Background setting is distinguished from contextual conditions used in practitioner judgment. Explicit interpretations and specific support choices remain optional; eligibility, search expressions and title-and-abstract screening rules are unchanged.

Supplementary materials will be added only when required by reporting guidelines or the target journal.
